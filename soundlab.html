Lock tone candidates (SPEC 2.11)

The problem: a lock in combat lasts seconds and the solid 880 Hz tone suits it; a lock on a sector beacon or a mining rock can last a whole flight, and the same tone is harsh over minutes. Combat keeps the solid tone either way. These three are candidates for what sector and mining get instead. Each plays for a few seconds then stops itself.

Vortex orbit demo (ideas6)

Brian's eight vortex recordings (audio/quadrant/vortex), each looping on its own HRTF panner and orbiting the listener at its own radius and speed — four one way, four the other. Start it, then put focus in the box: Up and Down raise or lower every orbit, Left and Right slow or speed them all up, Escape stops. The status line below the box reads back each change.

Arrow keys work here once the orbit is running.
