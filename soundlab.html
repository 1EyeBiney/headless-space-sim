Phase 3L — the HRTF laboratory

Brian's ten-point review of this page as an HRTF demonstrator (ideas_crazy_7.txt) and his answers (ideas8.txt) — only the five items he bracketed. Each demo below stops every other one when it starts, and any demo that turns the listener puts it back facing forward on stop, since the AudioListener is global.

Position Explorer (Phase 3L, L.1)

One continuous sound, moved by fixed steps rather than a continuous orbit — the way to hear exactly what the spatializer can do, and the prototype of the galactic map's own sound cursor (A.13): steer a sound to a point, then "select" it. Start it, then put focus in the box: Left/Right rotate it around you 45° at a time, Up/Down move it 30° above or below, Page Up/Down move it 100 farther or closer, Shift plus any of those is the fine step (15°, 10°, 25), Home resets to dead ahead, Enter "selects" the current position, Escape stops.

Arrow keys, Page Up/Down, Home, and Enter work here once the explorer is running.

3D grid cursor (ideas9, L.1b)

Brian's own idea for the galactic map's cursor, tried alongside L.1's polar one rather than in place of it: a 3D coordinate grid stepped through with the arrows and W/S, the coordinates spoken as it moves so the cursor is always on a grid point. Left/Right step left/right, Up/Down step up/down (height), W steps forward, S steps back — each a `gridStep` of 100; Shift plus any of those is the fine step, 25. Coordinates are read back as grid-cell counts from the origin ("2 right, 1 up, 3 forward"), rounded to the nearest 100-unit cell even after a fine Shift step (a fine adjustment can sit off the coarse grid; the reading rounds rather than showing a fraction). Home resets to the origin, Enter "selects," Escape stops. Brian: "If there are other ways to do this better, I am open" — this is the first thing to try, not the last.

Arrow keys, W, S, Home, and Enter work here once the grid cursor is running.

Flyby (Phase 3L, L.4)

Re-cast by Brian (ideas9): the four routes now loop audio/demo/propeller_plane5–8.mp3 (the last four, 12 seconds each — clips 1–4 are off this demo entirely). The four stunts — a close pass, an overhead dive, a corkscrew, an underneath pass, cycling one at a time — play no recording at all any more: a synthesized propeller (a sawtooth buzz plus a pulsed noise bed) follows the stunt's own speed instead. No Doppler.

Spatial audio room (Phase 3L, L.8)

Re-cast by Brian (ideas9): four real game sounds — an asteroid loop, a propeller plane, a mining laser, a vortex — placed randomly (position and distance) on every run, all playing at once. No quiz any more: every 10 seconds the room announces a turn ("Turning left"), turns you 90° in the same direction each time, then reports your own facing relative to where you started and where each sound is now ("The asteroid is now behind you").

Press Start test.

Lighthouse gate (Phase 3L, L.9)

A directional source — PannerNode orientation and a sound cone, loudest when its beam points at you. This is the same voice and the same rotation the game's Jump Gate now uses (space_station6, a 10-second sweep).

Left/Right and Page Up/Down work here once the lighthouse is running.

Lock tone candidates (SPEC 2.11)

The problem: a lock in combat lasts seconds and the solid 880 Hz tone suits it; a lock on a sector beacon or a mining rock can last a whole flight, and the same tone is harsh over minutes. Combat keeps the solid tone either way. These three are candidates for what sector and mining get instead. Each plays for a few seconds then stops itself.

3D vortex (ideas6, reshaped by Phase 3L, L.5)

Brian's eight vortex recordings (audio/quadrant/vortex), each looping on its own HRTF panner and orbiting the listener at its own radius and speed — four one way, four the other. L.5 gave each vortex its own shaping, one at a time; Brian's ideas9 note, after hearing it, asked for group controls back. Start it, then put focus in the box: Up/Down change the shared height, Page Up/Down change the shared vertical wobble, Home/End tilt the orbital ring from flat (around you) toward vertical (rising and falling in front), Left/Right change the shared speed, [ and ] change how many of the eight are audible (muted ones keep orbiting silently), R resets everything, Escape stops. The status line reads back all of it after every change.

Arrow keys, Page Up/Down, Home, End, [ and ], and R work here once the orbit is running.
